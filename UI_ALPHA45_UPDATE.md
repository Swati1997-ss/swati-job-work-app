# UI Alpha 45 Update

## Oil Mill stock and costing

- Groundnut purchase supports kg, 20 kg mann, and bags with kg per bag.
- Stores supplier, village, market yard, quality, mobile, expected Oil/Khol ratios, transport, brokerage, and other landed costs.
- Shows goods value, total landed cost, cost per kg/mann/bag, paid amount, and outstanding.
- Groundnut purchases and production batches can be edited; linked stock records update instead of duplicating.
- Raw stock shows kg, mann, and estimated bags with a detailed purchase ledger.
- Removed the separate Finished Oil card. Ready Oil displays actual full 15 kg tins plus loose kg.
- Khol combines company production and job-work purchases in one stock ledger with source labels.
- Empty-tin history identifies purchases, production usage, and job-work sales.
- General stock adjustment is no longer linked from the Oil Mill stock screen.

## Ratio analytics

- A small Ratio icon opens internal analytics; ratio values do not fill the main stock screen.
- Shows weighted expected Oil/Khol ratios and actual batch Oil/Khol/Process Difference ratios.
- Process Difference remains analysis only and is not stock.

## Grain/Pulse

- Waste/Reject is restored only for Grain/Pulse company processing and job-work purchases.
- Grain/Pulse Waste/Reject can be stocked and sold from its own sale mode.
- Oil Mill customer work remains free of any Waste feature.
