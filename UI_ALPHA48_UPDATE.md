# UI Alpha 48 Update

## Mobile interface
- Header branding is now larger, English, and no longer shows Sultanpur.
- Home labels are clearer in Gujarati and use larger app-friendly typography.
- Purchase replaces Customers in the bottom navigation.
- The Settings save button stays inside its form instead of floating over the header.

## Staff and Finance
- Staff ledger supports Salary, જમા and ઉધાર entries with a live current balance.
- Cash/Bank decreases for salary or ઉધાર and increases for Staff જમા.
- Finance includes an Assets register for cold-storage stock, machinery, property, deposits and other locked investments.
- Detailed Finance sections open from compact icon panels.

## Reports
- The overview title is changed to ધંધાની સ્થિતિ.
- Every report has a small chart icon.
- Every report detail includes a graphical comparison panel generated from the filtered data.

## Settings and synchronization
- Operators, Device & Audit, and Offline & Shared Sync use compact expandable panels.
- The Install App card is removed.
- Configured shared sync checks every 30 seconds and performs Sync & Pull when the two-minute interval is due.
- Returning to the app also checks whether an automatic sync is due.
