# UI Alpha 47 Update

## Oil and Khol sales
- Oil Sales is available beside Khol Sales.
- Sales can be marked Retail or Bulk/regular party.
- kg, mann, ton and 15 kg tin units are supported where appropriate.
- Transportation, loading and unloading costs are recorded separately.
- Oil/Khol stock is reduced by the normalized sold quantity; filled-tin stock is also reduced for tin sales.

## Khol batch analysis
- The standalone Batch menu is removed.
- Batch and profit analysis now lives inside Oil/Khol Sales.
- From/To dates automatically match Groundnut purchases and Khol sales.
- Batch data is available in Batch Excel and the Reports menu.

## Cleaner interface
- Retail history and Retail customers are collapsed behind small icon panels.
- Reports are grouped into Daily Operations, Production & Stock, and More Reports.
- The unused Price & Profit feature is removed.
- Operators and Device & Audit are compact expandable Settings panels.

## Automatic shared sync
- When shared sync is configured and the internet is available, Sync & Merge (including Pull Latest) runs every five minutes.
- Data changes still schedule a quick background sync.
- Manual controls remain available as a fallback.
