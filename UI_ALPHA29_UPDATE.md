# Swati App — UI Alpha 29

## Owner Home Dashboard

The Home screen now provides a compact owner-first business view.

### Money position
Home shows:
- Liquid Money
- Receivables
- Payables
- Stock Value

### Today's business
Today-only summary:
- Sales
- Purchases
- Expenses
- Job Work

### Production snapshot
Shows:
- Oil produced
- Khol produced
- Grain/Pulse processed output
- Grain/Pulse waste/reject output

### Needs Attention
Owner alerts include:
- Receivables
- Payables
- Zero/negative stock items
- Staff salary outstanding

Each alert opens the relevant module.

### Quick owner links
Direct shortcuts:
- Finance
- Costing
- Stock
- Reports
- Invoices
- Staff

The dashboard is intentionally compact and does not replace the detailed Finance or Reports modules.
