# UI Alpha 46 Update

## Oil Mill job-work entry
- Customer groundnut material is separated into Shing/Pods, Dana/Grains and Fada/Splits.
- Every material has its own quantity and kg/mann unit.
- The three weights are normalized to kilograms for output validation and settlement calculations.
- Existing Alpha records remain readable through automatic compatibility mapping.

## Card, PDF and sharing order
- The settlement table now shows Khol purchase first and job-work charges second.
- New tins and extra oil sales follow afterward.
- WhatsApp settlement text uses the same order and includes the final direction, paid amount, balance, status and delivery date.

## Mobile layout
- Oil Total and Job-work Charge are separate balanced summary cards.
- Labels and values no longer collide on narrow screens.
